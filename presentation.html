01 — The Idea
The Reality of Student Life & The Calmspace Idea
The Silent Student Struggle
  • Academic Overload: Constant deadlines, lab reports, exams, and performance anxiety.
  • Sleep Disruption: Irregular sleep compounding emotional fatigue and academic stress.
  • Hesitation & Stigma: Students avoid clinical portals due to fear of judgment or pathologization.
  • The SaaS Tracker Trap: Conventional apps use toxic streaks and clinical diagnostic forms that induce more anxiety.
What Calmspace Delivers
  • 30-Second Guided Check-Ins: Single-step rating of mood, stress, and sleep in a calm environment.
  • Empathetic Reflection: Understanding personal trends without being judged or diagnosed.
  • Pacing & Balance: Study task checklist paired with an integrated 5-minute breather timer.
  • Immediate Human Care: Direct connection to verified national helplines and campus psychologists.
  • Not Medical Software: Positioned deliberately as a comforting companion, never clinical software.

04 — Architecture
Full-Stack System Architecture & Tech Stack
Frontend (Client)
  • React 19 & TypeScript: Strictly typed, reactive single-page app.
  • Vite Dev & Build: Sub-second HMR and production bundle optimization.
  • Tailwind CSS: Centralized token architecture with custom utility classes.
  • Recharts: Accessible, responsive wellbeing trend visualizations.
  • Three.js / WebGL: Interactive 3D volumetric mascot controller.
Backend (FastAPI)
  • Python & FastAPI: High-performance async REST API with auto-OpenAPI docs.
  • Pydantic v2: Strict input validation and request/response contracts.
  • Pure Safety Engine: Deterministic, zero-I/O mathematical routing module.
  • AI Adapter Layer: Gemini / Mock provider abstraction with validation filters.

Data & Security
  • Anonymous Sessions: Indexed by UUID token; zero roll numbers or emails.
  • SQLite with WAL: Fast, persistent storage surviving restarts.
  • Zero-Storage Reflections: Reflections processed in memory, never written to disk.
  • 2-Step Data Erasure: Instant transactional purge across all tables.
  • CI Automation: GitHub Actions validating 226 tests on every PR.
05 — Safety Architecture
Safety Routing Architecture & The AI Bypass Rule
Deterministic Safety Routing Matrix
  • LOW ROUTE (Doing Okay): Mood ≥ 3, Stress ≤ 2, Sleep ≥ 6.0h. Soft Sage badge (#DCE9DF). Delivers 3–5 sentence supportive encouragement and 2 pacing chips.
  • MEDIUM ROUTE (Worth Support): Mood = 2, Stress 3–4, Sleep < 6.0h, or declining trend. Grounding reflection, box-breathing, and campus counselling link.
  • HIGH ROUTE (Crisis Escalation): Mood = 1, Stress = 5, Sleep < 3.5h, or crisis keywords. Calm High-Contrast Plum/Ink screen. AI IS 100% BYPASSED.
  • THE STRICT AI BYPASS GUARANTEE: Zero LLM calls on high risk. We never let an AI hallucinate or improvise when human safety is at stake.
Engine Protection
  • Multilingual Crisis Corpus: Trained on English, Hindi, Devanagari, and Hinglish distress phrases.
  • Negation Isolation: Sentence-level parsing prevents false negatives on negated clauses.
  • Immutable Engine: safety_evaluator.py locked to SHA 8f5e2e98... backed by 156 tests.
06 — Privacy Safeguards
Privacy by Design & Data Safeguards
Structural Architectural Privacy
  • No Intrusive Identity: Students interact through an anonymous UUID session token. Zero roll numbers, emails, or names required.
  • Zero-Storage Reflections: Reflection evaluated in volatile memory, then discarded. No 'reflection' column exists in database tables.
  • Strict Session Isolation: Session A can never query, read, toggle, or delete Session B's check-ins or study tasks.
  • Explicit AI Consent: Untoggling AI consent omits free-text reflection from AI processing entirely.
Student Control & Data Deletion
  • 2-Step Permanent Purge: Clicking 'Delete all my data' triggers an atomic DELETE across all tables, permanently purging check-ins, tasks, and settings.
  • Safe Error Responses: Database failure responses never leak SQL statements, stack traces, or credentials to the client.
  • Zero Ad Trackers: No commercial analytics, third-party cookies, or marketing trackers.
  • Non-Diagnostic Language: Personal metrics are descriptive pacing reflections, never clinical diagnoses.
