How It Works

Three simple steps, zero pressure

Check in whenever you like. We don't build streaks, send guilt-inducing notifications, or demand daily loyalty.

1. Check in for one minute

Rate your mood, sleep duration, and current stress on a clean 1-to-5 scale. An optional private reflection is available if you wish to write.

2. Get gentle guidance

When your baseline is steady or mildly stressed, receive 3-5 sentences of grounding reflection and a couple of non-demanding suggestions.

3. Reach real people

If your stress is intense or distress indicators appear, AI is bypassed entirely. You are immediately connected to verified human counsellors.

Safety You Can Inspect

Rules decide safety. AI writes the language. Humans help.

Calmspace is a supportive companion, not therapy, medicine, or a clinical diagnosis system. Our safety routing is completely deterministic.

1

Rules decide safety

Deterministic logic written in Python evaluates risk thresholds. An algorithm or AI never guesses whether a student is safe.

2

AI writes the language

For Low and Medium routes only, AI crafts comforting words of reflection. It is strictly barred from offering medical diagnoses or prescribing remedies.

3

Humans help

When distress is detected, AI steps aside completely. The student is presented with direct tap-to-call links to trained national and campus crisis teams.

Clear Boundary: If you are experiencing a severe mental health emergency, please dial 14416 (Tele-MANAS) or 112 immediately.

Privacy by Design

Your thoughts belong to you alone

We designed Calmspace so that we cannot see, sell, or lose your private identity.

Anonymous nickname only

No email address, student ID, Google account, or phone number required. You choose what to call yourself (or stay as "Student").

Reflections never stored

Any written reflection you share during check-in is discarded immediately after processing. Only numeric ratings (mood, stress, sleep) stay on record.

Zero third-party trackers

We load no advertising scripts, no analytics beacons, and no external cookie trackers. Fonts are self-hosted locally on our server.

One-click complete erasure

Visit Settings anytime to delete all stored check-ins and preferences in two clicks. The server immediately purges all trace of your anonymous session.
